Case Timeline Builder

Turns case notes into a clean, numbered, chronological timeline and flags unusually large gaps between entries. The notes below are prefilled from this case's full account history — edit, add, or remove lines as needed, then rebuild. Tolerates varied date formats and separators, not one rigid template. Times are shown in your local timezone.

Case Notes

Each line should start with a date (e.g. 8/28, 8/28/2026, or Aug 28, 2026) and, optionally, a time (e.g. 6:41pm or 18:41) — a dash, colon, comma, or just a space can separate the timestamp from the note. Oldest or newest first, either way; they're sorted automatically. Reset to this case's notes if you've edited the text above and want to start over.
